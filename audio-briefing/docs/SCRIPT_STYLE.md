# Korean broadcast script style guide (original synthesis)

Objective: sound like a clear, professional Korean morning bulletin, not like a written newspaper summary, and not an imitation of an identifiable broadcaster, presenter or copyrighted script.

## Style reference corpus

Use **permissioned** broadcast scripts or original/appropriately licensed reporting. A sensible first pass is a varied corpus by topic (economy, international, society, everyday life), with distinct anchors versus field-reporter formats. Analyze patterns into aggregate rules rather than keeping passages to reuse. Do not assume that a public news site authorizes mass scraping, model training, or verbatim redistribution.

Extract:
- Opening: lead with the specific confirmed development, not dramatic filler.
- Pace: a single fact per spoken sentence, comfortable breath pauses.
- Form: what happened -> essential context -> confirmed impact -> what is not known.
- Spoken numbers: write amounts and dates in a form that is unambiguous aloud.
- Attribution: identify who said what; separate statements, forecasts and verified facts.
- Register: restrained formal Korean ending (e.g. -습니다, -했습니다), no clickbait.
- Transitions: short bridges between topics; do not insert ungrounded commentary.
- Ending: close simply; avoid predictions unless a source clearly supports them.

## Editorial quality bar

1. Every substantive fact in a segment must be traceable to permitted evidence, with citation metadata kept outside the spoken text.
2. No unsupported causality, fabricated quotes, unnamed sources, fabricated dates or embellished numbers.
3. For contested claims use attribution: '기관은 ...라고 밝혔습니다'; label projections and uncertainty.
4. Readable aloud: avoid stacking numbers, abbreviations and nested subordinate clauses.
5. No direct lifting of signature copy or long strings from a single broadcaster.
6. Script is regenerated from **today's facts** and the **style rules**; example scripts may guide rhythm only.
7. Never generate an exact presenter voice. Use an original licensed synthetic voice, and disclose it.

## Short fictional example (NOT REAL NEWS)

Text-only example of rhythm: '가상의 도시에서 새 교통 안내 서비스를 시험합니다. 운영 기관은 다음 달부터 일부 노선을 대상으로 시범 운영할 계획이라고 밝혔습니다. 다만 실제 도입 범위와 요금은 아직 확정되지 않았습니다.'

This is a made-up writing example, not a factual headline. Production must not read it as news.
